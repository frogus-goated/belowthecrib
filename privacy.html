Privacy Policy

Below the Crib Discord bot. Last updated October 19, 2026.

Short version

The bot only uses what Discord gives it to run the server. We don't sell your data, share it for ads, or use it for anything outside the Below the Crib server.

What the bot can see

  • Your Discord user ID, username and the roles you have in the server.
  • Messages in channels the bot can read, when it needs them to run a command or help with moderation.
  • Server details like channels, roles and settings.

What we keep

Only what the bot needs to work, for example which ping roles you picked or a moderation note. We don't keep copies of your messages beyond what Discord itself stores.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data. We only share it if the law requires it. The bot runs on Discord, so Discord's Privacy Policy also applies.

Your choices

You can leave the server at any time, and you can ask us to delete anything the bot stored about you. Ask in the support channel and we'll remove it.

Kids

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or the minimum age in your country). The bot is not meant for anyone younger.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Ask in the support channel of the Below the Crib Discord server.
